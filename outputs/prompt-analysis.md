# Prompt Analysis

## Objective

The objective of this prompt was to generate authentic UGC-style advertisements for RACE's Fuel Injection Cleaning service.

## Why Customer Persona Was Included

The customer persona helps the AI create realistic stories and experiences instead of generic promotional content.

## Why Pain Points Were Included

Pain points such as poor mileage, rough engine sound, and reduced pickup help the AI focus on real customer problems.

## Why Desired Results Were Included

Desired results guide the AI toward highlighting customer benefits such as better fuel efficiency, smoother driving, and lower maintenance costs.

## Why UGC Structure Was Included

The Hook → Problem → Solution → Result → CTA framework helps create engaging and conversion-focused ad content.

## Prompt Engineering Approach

The prompt combines:

* Business Context
* Customer Persona
* Pain Points
* Desired Results
* UGC Ad Framework
* Platform Requirements

This structure helps generate authentic, reusable, and high-converting UGC advertisements.
